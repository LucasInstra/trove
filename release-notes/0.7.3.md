# trove 0.7.3

Goals get a new look, and the website always opens light.

## Goals

- **3D icons for goals.** Goals use the same 3D icons as the rest of the app, with 40 new ones to pick from: bike, camping, passport, solar energy, savings jar, sneakers, sailboat and more. Goals you already have keep their icon.
- **Empty lists.** The planning screens show a small wallet instead of a plus sign when there is nothing yet.

## Website

- **Always light.** The website opens in the light theme whatever your system prefers. The theme button still switches to dark for the visit.
