# trove 0.7.7

A screen of its own for the house, and people who own their income.

## New

- **Housing screen.** Housing has its own entry in the menu, with the housing card, rent and bills, people, the split and the housing category, all in one place.
- **Add and remove people.** The house is no longer limited to two: add up to six people and remove them again. Removing someone hands their bills back to you.
- **Whose income.** Every income source says whose it is. What each person earns is the sum of their sources, so there is a single place to type it, and the house is split in proportion.
- **Who pays.** Any bill can say who pays it, not only the rent.
- **Split for the whole house.** With more than two people the split shows everyone and who gets money back or owes.

## Sample data

- No more 13th salary in December, since not everyone gets one.

## Also in this release

- Settings no longer holds the housing category; it lives in Housing.
