# trove 0.1.3

The keeper moved out, and the dashboard grew up: headlines with context, installment tracking, money other people owe you, hand-entered entries, and a strategy section with net worth, health, debts and retirement.

## No more mascot

- **The piggy is gone.** The canvas mascot, its customisation sheet and its settings are removed. The forecast card keeps the small 3D piggy as its illustration.

## Month at a glance

- **Four headline numbers.** Income, spending and balance carry their change against the previous month, and a fourth tile shows what installments ending this month free up.
- **Free balance hero.** The forecast bar now splits into gastos pessoais, moradia, reservado and a receber, with the free remainder never funded by money others still owe you.

## Bills, cards and split that explain themselves

- **Vencidas / Em dia.** The bills card filters by status, and by Tudo / Pessoal / Da casa from your housing category.
- **Your share on every bill.** With two or more people, each row shows sua parte under the total, following the same rule the split settles by.
- **Cards with identity.** The invoice figure names whose card it is and whether the month is open or closed.
- **Installments.** Bills can carry a parcel count (9/24); the run derives from the start month, no row per parcel is stored, and a dashboard card tracks what is left plus the "Compras parceladas" block.
- **A receber.** Debts other people owe you are their own records: register them, mark each one received on its own, delete if needed.

## Manual entries and a tidier month

- **Lançar.** The top bar has a global add button: type, description, amount, date, account and category land straight in the ledger, outside the OFX dedupe which stays import-only.
- **Limpar mês.** The ledger header can reset a month to its imported state: hand-entered rows and paid marks go, imports and debts never move.
- **Greeting and profile.** The top bar greets you by time of day with your name, settable under Ajustes → Perfil.

## Charts and strategy

- **Six months by category.** The dashboard chart stacks each month by category with a legend and per-slice tooltips, and the cash-flow chart tooltips show in, out and balance.
- **PF Score.** On-time bills, margin and reserve months in a 0–100 gauge with a one-line verdict naming the weakest part.
- **Compras parceladas e metas com aporte.** Reserve goals accept hand-entered contributions that move the progress bar, and categories can carry monthly budgets shown as spent / cap in red when over.
- **Visão estratégica.** New sidebar section: Receitas e gastos (income sources plus per-category spending with budget bars), Patrimônio (reserves plus a 12-month accumulated curve), Saúde (the score plus a five-point checkup), Dívidas (open installments with payoff forecast plus open invoices), and Aposentadoria (a contribution simulator with a projection curve and honest assumptions).
