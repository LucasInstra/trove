# trove 0.7.0

Bank-style cards, smoother motion, and a fix for dragging dashboard cards.

## Cards

- **Bank looks.** Cards can wear the look of Nubank, Itaú, Santander, Bradesco, Caixa, Inter, PicPay, C6, Banco do Brasil, Mercado Pago and XP, each with its own versions (Ultravioleta, Black, Platinum and so on).
- **Bank and network marks.** The card shows a mark for the bank and the card network (Mastercard, Visa, Elo, Amex, Hipercard) where a real card prints them.
- **Card editor.** Pick the bank by its mark, then pick one of that bank's card versions. A new card takes the bank look from the name you type.

## Overview

- **Dragging cards** into empty space now moves the card instead of leaving it dimmed.
- **Housing meter** is green under the ceiling and red over it.
- **Spending chart** no longer clips the small segments.

## Motion

- The eye button blurs the numbers out and back, and the icon blinks.
- Changing theme crossfades, changing month slides the title, and the bell rings when it opens.
- The transaction checkbox appears when you hover a row.

## Dialogs and settings

- A dialog no longer closes when a text selection ends outside it, and tall dialogs scroll instead of running off the screen.
- Language now sits with Appearance in Settings, and the sample-data option moved next to About.
- The OFX/CSV switch on Import is larger.
