# trove 0.5.0

Numbers that show their source, and a gentler first run.

## Provenance

- **Where does this come from?** An info button on every transaction, on the month summary of the ledger and on the KPI card opens the file (OFX or CSV) or the manual entry behind the number, with the account and the import date.

## Import

- **Four steps.** Choose, preview, import, summary, with a progress bar.
- **Preview before writing.** Each OFX file shows its account, period, how many entries are new, how many are already stored and the first rows. Nothing is saved until you confirm.
- **Summary.** The final report also says how many entries your rules categorised.

## First run

- **Welcome screen.** With no accounts yet, the Overview opens on a dark welcome with three polaroids and two ways forward: import a statement or try the sample data. It can be skipped and does not come back.

## Settings

- **Grouped in tabs.** Account and data, Appearance, Language, Updates, About.
- **Theme in Settings** and a button to copy the database path.

## Transactions and categories

- **Category chips** tinted at 15% of the category colour.
- **Floating action bar** that follows the window while rows are selected, plus "Select all".
- **Category tree** with any depth, indented, with an elbow to each parent and a sub-category count.
