# trove 0.8.3

Spending sheet with your entries, optional crypto tracking and card invoice payments.

## New

- **Optional crypto tracking.** Hold Bitcoin, Ethereum and other coins in Investments, with the price updated by itself. It is off by default: the app asks once, and you can turn it on or off in Settings > Account and data. When on, it only asks CoinGecko for the current price of the coins you hold (just the coin names, never your amounts); when off, no price request is made. The holding form is simpler for crypto: coin, units, purchase date and amount paid.
- **Paying a card invoice.** "Mark as paid" asks which account paid and on which day, records the payment as a transfer under the Card category, and shows the invoice as zero due with the amount that was paid. Undoing removes the payment.
- **Spending sheet shows your entries.** Spreadsheet mode opens on Spending, and the month's ledger entries appear in Income and Expenses, with the date, an editable paid checkbox and a pencil to edit.

## Improved

- **Income from that month on.** Editing a salary in one month no longer changes the others; it applies from that month forward. New bills start in the month you are viewing.
- **Cash flow counts the salary.** The charts and the month summary count the recurring income as money in, and the spending tile now shows what was spent plus the bills still to pay.
- **Recurring charges.** Income is no longer listed as a recurring charge, and any charge can be hidden.
- **More rows when taller.** "Where the money went" and "Latest transactions" show more rows when you resize them taller.

## Fixed

- The income total no longer doubles in the Spending sheet, and expense paid checkboxes work.
