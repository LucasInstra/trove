# trove 0.8.2

See what is inside "Others" in the investment charts.

## Improved

- **"Show all" on folded charts.** Invested and return, where the money is, by bank and maturities by year now have a "Show all" button that opens the full list (scrollable), and "Show fewer" folds it back.
- **What is inside "Others" in stacked charts.** When many owners or types are folded into one grey color, an "Others (N)" item lists them with their values.
