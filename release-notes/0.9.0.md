# trove 0.9.0

Several accounts as an option, crypto gains, a calendar in the title and a lot of polish.

## New

- **Several accounts, if you want them.** Off by default. Turn it on in Settings, in the invitation banner or during the introduction. Then every figure can be shown for all accounts or for one, each account shows what came in and went out in the month, and an opening balance is optional.
- **An Accounts card.** Each account's result for the month, what is left of the month with the salary, and the account that receives the salary is yours to choose.
- **Crypto.** Holdings show what was paid, what they are worth now and the gain or loss. Prices are an optional connection, and refreshing asks first.
- **A day and a month in the title.** Click the day to change only the day, click the month to change the month and the year. New manual entries start on the day you picked.
- **Display currency.** Pick the symbol amounts are shown with in Settings. Nothing is converted.
- **What is new after an update.** The update notes follow the app's language, and the first time you open a new version a short list of highlights appears.
- **Invoices that grew.** A paid card invoice that gets a new or edited purchase shows the difference and offers to pay it. The bell warns about invoices that are due, overdue or grew.
- **Goals tied to an account**, shown on the Overview and Plans screens.
- **A short tip on each screen**, with a "Got it" button, and a new introduction step about accounts.

## Improved

- Every input, select list and checkbox shares the app's look.
- You can add a manual entry from an empty ledger, and the first one creates the main account.
- The paid column of the spending sheet is easier to read, and the cards card uses less space.
- A card with a chosen height grows with its content instead of scrolling.
- A manual entry with no amount says so instead of doing nothing.
