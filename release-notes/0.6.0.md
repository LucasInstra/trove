# trove 0.6.0

A calmer, flatter look, and a few things you asked for.

## Look

- **One data palette.** Category colours follow a single blue ramp (five steps, then slate) instead of a rainbow. Spending and housing share one vivid red.
- **One type scale.** Seven sizes, nothing heavier than semibold, and quieter cents on every amount.
- **Flat panels.** Four corner radii, white cards without tints, a pale blue border on hover, and no card shadow in the dark theme.
- **Dark theme** with a new near-black palette, flat top bar and an accent-outlined lead card.
- **Top bar.** Only the month sits in a pill, with a stable arrow position, a custom month picker and the brush stroke under the month.
- **Sidebar.** Roomier rows, a soft active pill and an animation of its own on every icon.
- **Icons.** A new set of 3D icons, with no repeats between cards.

## Overview

- **Forecast balance first**, on the left, and its legend no longer spills out of its chips.
- **Latest transactions** shows four rows and a "See all" link.
- **Where the money went** shows five legend rows, the rest grouped as "Other".
- **Bills to pay** keeps one filter visible; scope and due days live under "More filters".
- **Income vs spending** has a fixed chart height.
- **Retirement chart** shows the value on hover.
- **KPI sparklines** are drawn solid, with no gaps.

## Housing

- The ceiling meter is green under the income ceiling and vivid red over it.

## Profile and privacy

- **Initials** are the first letters of the first and last name, two at most.
- **Profile photo** can be chosen in Settings, cropped square and stored locally.
- **Eye button** in the top bar hides every money amount until you open it again.
