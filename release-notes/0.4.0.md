# trove 0.4.0

A redesign: warm frame, cold data.

## Look

- **One token file.** `src/styles/tokens.css` holds every colour, radius, shadow and font for light and dark. A single `--primary` (`#1846e1`, `#6d9bff` in dark) and one card shadow.
- **Bundled fonts.** Bricolage Grotesque for titles and the big numbers, Inter for the interface, Gloria Hallelujah for polaroid captions. All local, no external requests.
- **Editorial pieces.** Dark anchor card for the month forecast, icon bubbles around the 3D icons, paper bands, polaroids on the plan screens and empty states, a brush underline on the month, a floating pill top bar.
- **Credit cards.** Trove blue and graphite are now the first two designs. Cards you already coloured keep their colour.
- **Accessibility.** Visible focus ring on every control, token pairs checked at 4.5:1 or better in both themes (`node scripts/check-contrast.mjs`).

## Navigation

- Main group has 6 items, with the rest under "Manage", so Settings no longer appears twice.
- Housing is now a tab inside Plans.

## Removed

- The "Visual: novo / antes" title-bar switch and the Apple/Visor stylesheets.
