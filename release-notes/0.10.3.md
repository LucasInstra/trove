# trove 0.10.3

The investment sheet counts days correctly when you look at another month, plus dark-theme touch-ups.

## Fixed

- **Days in the investment sheet.** Looking at a later month now counts up to the same day of that month (24 Nov to 24 Dec is 30 days), not to the month's last day.
- **Maturity from a term.** A term that ends in a shorter month stops on its last day (31 Jan plus 1 month is 28/29 Feb, not 3 Mar), in the sheet and in the charts' projection.
- **Today after 9 pm.** The investment sheet and Net worth no longer jump to the next day late in the evening.
- **Dark theme.** The import drop area and the investment sheet's header are dark again, and the import steps line up with the title above them.
