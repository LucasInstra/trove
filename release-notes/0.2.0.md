# trove 0.2.0

The ledger grows up: imports in two formats, transfers and refunds that keep the totals honest, auto-categorization, monthly budgets with pace, a recurrence radar, charts with real data behind them, tags, holdings and a monthly report.

## Imports

- **CSV joins OFX.** Bank CSVs import with a per-bank mapping (separator, encoding, date and decimal formats, one amount column or a debit/credit pair), a live preview of parsed and skipped lines, and saved profiles per bank. Reimports land on duplicates, and OFX/CSV overlaps share the same fingerprint.
- **Export and backup.** The filtered ledger exports to CSV in the same dialect the import reads, and the database backs up with `VACUUM INTO` plus a validated restore that heals older backups on boot.

## Money that moves

- **Transfers.** Two accounts, one event: a debit/credit pair that stays out of income, spending and categories, and only vanishes together.
- **Refunds.** A mirrored entry that nets against its own side instead of inflating the other, one per original, no chains.
- **Rules.** Substring matching with amount windows and priority auto-categorizes every import without ever overwriting an explicit choice.

## Planning

- **Monthly budgets.** Per-category caps or a whole-month cap, carryover that rolls the unused part forward, and a pace readout against the day of the month, plus free-to-spend.
- **Recurrence radar.** Subscriptions hiding in the ledger surface as due, overdue or scheduled.
- **Cash forecast.** 3/6/12-month projection repeating the trailing average, on the flow screen.

## Reading the money

- **Charts.** Daily burn against the budget pace, sparklines on every KPI, a running-net trajectory on the series card, and a breakdown that follows the hover.
- **Monthly report.** One page with totals, savings rate, top spending, broken caps and due subscriptions, with copy to clipboard.
- **Tags and bulk edit.** Free-form tags with ledger filter, multi-select, bulk categorization and bulk tagging.
- **Holdings.** Off-ledger assets join reserves and the statement curve into one net worth.

## Repairs

- Databases stamped ahead of their columns heal on open, and every migration stays additive and idempotent.
