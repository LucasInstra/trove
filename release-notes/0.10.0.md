# trove 0.10.0

Credit cards can follow the month you pay them, and AI assistants get a guide to your data.

## New

- **Count card purchases in the month the invoice is due.** In a card's settings, turn on "Count purchases in the month the invoice is due". A purchase on 5 Nov on a card that closes on the 24th and is due on the 1st then counts in December: in the totals, the charts, the budgets and the spreadsheet. It is off by default, so cards you leave alone keep counting by purchase date.
- **Invoices in spreadsheet mode.** With that option on, the card's purchases show up in the Budget sheet as one invoice row with its due date. Click the name to see the purchases. Ticking it as paid opens "Pay invoice", which records the payment as a transfer from your account to the card, so the money is not counted twice.
- **A guide for AI assistants.** The data folder (`%APPDATA%\trove`) now has `LEIA-ME-PARA-IA.md`. It explains the database and gives safe recipes: change categories, add or delete an entry, and turn an invoice payment recorded as an expense into a transfer. Close trove and copy `trove.db` before letting an assistant change it.

## Fixed

- Archived accounts leave the totals, charts and recurrences, not only the pickers.
- Restoring a backup replaces the database in one step.
- Crypto prices refresh without freezing the window.
- Clearer messages when a bill, card or goal has an invalid name, day or amount.
