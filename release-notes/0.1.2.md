# trove 0.1.2

The dashboard learned to rearrange itself, and the ledger stepped out of the dashboard.

## The dashboard is yours

- **Cards move, resize and shrink.** Drag a card anywhere in the grid; the dashboard previews the new order before you let go. Width is a numbered control (one, two or three columns), and the size control names what it will do: compact, minimise, expand. A widened card does not stretch the same content: it shows more of it, with every card at once, the bills behind the housing total, the goals in two columns, and a year of spending instead of a semester.
- **Compact cards keep their figures.** Between the full card and the one line chip there is now a compact card with the same information in a smaller form: a progress bar for bills, the ceiling for housing, the split bar, the mini donut or the six bars.
- **Cards you do not need stay out.** The house split asks for a second person, the housing card asks for rent, and any card can be taken off the dashboard and waits in a shelf under the layout bar until it is added back.
- **Layout is remembered.** The order, the widths, the size and what is hidden live in the settings and survive a restart. A layout saved by an older version still opens.

## The ledger, on its own

- Lançamentos left the dashboard and got a screen with the search, the account filter and the category editor.
- **Planning screens.** Cartões, Reserva, Casa and Renda have their own tabs, and the editor in each one is the same dialog the dashboard uses, so a goal edited in Reserva is the goal the dashboard card shows.

## Charts and motion

- **Where the money went** is a stacked breakdown bar with the month total, the shares and a legend that lights the slice you point at.
- **Last months** highlights the current month and shows the year when the card is wide.
- Buttons and cards answer in 150 to 160ms with a short lift and a press of 0.97, and the whole language is switched off when the system asks for reduced motion.

## Interface

- The rail opens with your profile and groups the menu, the planning subjects and the settings. Below 768px it becomes a bottom nav.
- The window bar carries the app mark and name, and names the screen no longer.
- The 3D icon set from the spreadsheet is cut into transparent assets and used on the cards, the hero and the profile.
- English and Portuguese, including every new screen and string.

## Under the hood

- The shell migrated to Tailwind v4 with the old sheet kept in a lower layer, so the screens that were not migrated yet keep working untouched.
- The domain gained the layout model (validated), the row packing that closes the holes in the grid, and the planning summaries the new screens read. 103 unit tests.
- The installer is the MSI, with the NSIS setup and a portable zip beside it. The updater installs the same MSI.
- Everything runs offline. No financial data leaves the computer. The build is not code signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn on first run.
