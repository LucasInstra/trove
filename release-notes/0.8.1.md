# trove 0.8.1

Big portfolios: the investments sheet stays a manageable size with 100 or more holdings.

## New

- **More investment types.** The product list now has LC, LIG, Tesouro Selic, Tesouro IPCA+, Tesouro Prefixado, real estate funds, stocks, ETFs, BDRs, COE, pension and crypto, besides CDB, LCI, LCA and the rest.

## Improved

- **Long investment lists.** The table scrolls inside its card with the header and totals always in view, instead of making the page huge.
- **Charts with many items.** Bar charts and the distribution show the biggest groups and fold the rest into "Others (N)"; the returns chart shows the 11 biggest. Long names are cut with an ellipsis.
- **Narrow windows.** Card headers on the sheets wrap instead of pushing buttons and tabs out of the card.
