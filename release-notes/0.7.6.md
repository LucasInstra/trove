# trove 0.7.6

Delete what you add, calmer screens and brighter colors.

## New

- **Delete incomes and goals.** Both now have a Delete button in their edit dialog.
- **Erase my data.** Settings has a new card that wipes everything you entered, after you type a confirmation word. Categories and settings are kept.
- **Rule colors.** When you create or edit an auto-categorization rule you can pick the color of its category.

## Improved

- **Cards screen.** Installments and open invoices are now tidy tiles: parcel counter, amount, progress, payoff month, and each card's due day and share of the limit.
- **House.** The housing category setting moved to Settings, and the house screen now uses two stacks, so there is no blank gap under the housing card.
- **Cash forecast.** The 3 / 6 / 12 selector is fixed and the empty space under the chart is gone.
- **Income list.** Compact, two columns, no blank space under "Free to spend".
- **Trends.** Charts sit two by two.
- **Score ring.** The number and "/ 100" are bigger and centered.
- **Goals card.** More breathing room between name, amounts and bar.
- **Brighter colors.** The checkup and the spending by category use vivid status and category colors.

## Fixes

- Plus and minus signs sit next to the number, after the currency, and disappear when values are hidden.
