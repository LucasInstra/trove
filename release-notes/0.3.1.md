# trove 0.3.1

A visual pass over the whole app.

## Look

- **Apple system layer.** System colours, continuous corners, a translucent source-list sidebar with a tinted selection, filled fields with a focus ring, an iOS-style segmented control and thicker dialog material.
- **Flat pale-blue cards.** A white canvas, pale-blue cards with white tiles inside, royal blue as the one strong colour and 12px rectangular buttons. Dark mode keeps the system look.
- **Look switch.** A temporary "Visual: novo / antes" button in the title bar compares the new look with the previous one. The choice is remembered.
