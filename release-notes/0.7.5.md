# trove 0.7.5

Signs that stay next to the number, and tighter screens.

## Fixes

- **Sign placement.** Plus and minus now sit next to the number, after the currency, everywhere ("R$ -1.436,90", "R$ +85,00"), including the composition bar and chart tooltips.
- **Hidden values.** When you hide your numbers, the plus and minus signs are hidden too.
- **Income sources.** The list is compact and in two columns, so adding more incomes no longer leaves a blank gap under "Free to spend".
- **Trends.** The charts sit two by two instead of one per row, with less empty space.

## Also in this release

- Local tooling and screenshot folders are no longer tracked in the repository.
