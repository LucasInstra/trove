# trove 0.8.4

The ledger counts the salary and the crypto notice has to be answered.

## Improved

- **Salary in the ledger summary.** "In" in Transactions now includes the recurring income (the salary) of the month, with the entries.
- **Crypto notice.** The notice that asks about price tracking only closes with "Turn on" or "Not now": clicking outside or pressing Esc no longer dismisses it.
