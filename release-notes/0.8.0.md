# trove 0.8.0

Spreadsheet mode: investments and spending as editable sheets.

## New

- **Investments sheet.** Edit owner, institution, product, tax regime, maturity and notes in a table, with totals and charts (including yield by type) on the same tab.
- **Spending sheet.** The month as a budget: income sources, expenses with "paid", totals and the expected final balance. Add rows inline, edit cells in place, sort by header, filter paid or pending, and drag rows to reorder.
- **Partner expenses.** An option beside the tabs marks whose each income and expense is, colors names and values by person, and adds charts per person.
- **Person colors.** Pick a color for yourself and for each person in Housing; charts and lists follow it.
- **Receivables.** Who owes you is plain text (they need not be a household member), "received" is a simple checkbox, and each item can be edited or deleted from the card.
- **Housing bills.** Rent and household bills can be edited right in the card, and the change shows up everywhere.

## Improved

- **Dashboard default.** The default Overview arrangement (and "Reset layout") is now three balanced columns.
- **Messages.** Backend errors are shown in your language.
