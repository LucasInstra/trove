# trove 0.1.1

trove answers a second question: can I pay everything this month, and how much is left?

## The planner

- **Forecast card.** The month starts from the income, subtracts what is planned and shows what is left, with the composition bar split into housing, other bills and what stayed, each with its amount and share. The keeper sits in the same card.
- **Bills to pay.** Recurring bills with a due day, a month scoreboard ("2 of 8 paid"), day chips and a checkbox. Marking a bill paid is what moves the forecast, and unmarking puts it back.
- **Cards.** A credit card is the credit account that already comes from the OFX file. Set the limit, the closing day and the due day, and the invoice is computed from the window instead of typed: a purchase on the 4th lands in the current invoice, on the 6th in the next one.
- **Housing.** The share of the income that goes to housing, with a ceiling you can change and a marker on the meter.
- **Goals.** Reserve and building goals with a target and a monthly deposit. Progress comes only from bills already paid, so the bar cannot move on its own.
- **Cash flow.** Twelve months of money in and out, with the current month using the plan instead of the statement, plus the running balance.
- **Notifications.** A bell with the bills that are late or due within three days, card closings and the missing income. Only the read keys are stored.
- **Multi-account.** Import per account and filter the ledger by account. The same statement in two different accounts does not collide.

## Interface

- White cards on a light background, blue accent, three columns: the forecast takes two, bills one, then cards, housing and goals, then the charts.
- The headline number shows the cents smaller, like a price.
- Light and dark themes, remembered between sessions.
- English and Portuguese, including the new screens.

## Under the hood

- The database is versioned. Opening a 0.1.0 file migrates it in place: every row keeps its money, gains a `uid` and a `deleted_at`, and nothing is copied or dropped.
- Deleting a transaction frees its deduplication key, so reimporting the statement brings it back instead of being swallowed.
- The projection, the invoice window and the notifications are pure functions with their own tests.

## Notes

- Everything runs offline. No financial data leaves the computer.
- The installer is now an MSI, and the updater installs the same MSI. The NSIS setup and the portable zip stay in the release.
- The build is not code signed, so Windows SmartScreen may warn on first run.
