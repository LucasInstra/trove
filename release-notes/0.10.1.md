# trove 0.10.1

The card invoice option is easier to find, invoices read better in spreadsheet mode, and the AI guide explains what changed between versions.

## New

- **One switch for all cards.** The Cards screen now has "Count card purchases in the month the invoice is due" at the top, with an example using your card's own dates. Each card can still opt out in its edit screen.
- **Invoices in spreadsheet mode.** The invoice row stands out, with the card, due date and number of purchases, and its purchase list shows day, category and notes.
- **New entry from the spreadsheet.** "New entry" adds a one-off expense or income next to "Add fixed bill".
- **Late items in red.** "Left to pay, by due date" includes unpaid invoices and marks overdue ones in red.
- **An invoice paid in another month** leaves the balance and "Paid expenses" of the month it was paid.
- **Hide every tip at once** in Settings › Introduction.
- **AI guide:** a section on behaviour changes between versions, how to spot data recorded the old way and how to convert it, plus how to reload the window (Ctrl+Shift+R) to see changes made to the file.

## Fixed

- Notes on entries now show in the spreadsheet and in recent entries.
- Categories inside an invoice are translated.
- Spreadsheet numbers animate when they change.
