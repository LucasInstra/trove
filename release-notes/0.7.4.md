# trove 0.7.4

Better sample data, tidier dashboard defaults and small polish.

## Sample data

- **Hidden and removable.** "Try with sample data" is now a small link on the welcome screen, and Settings has a collapsed Sample data section with a button that removes only the examples.
- **A more realistic household.** Two incomes (three and two minimum wages), expenses sized to match, with months that save and months that overspend.
- **More to explore.** Investments, six installment purchases and auto-categorization rules.

## Polish

- **Dashboard.** Cards come grouped in a sensible default order, and the reset button now explains what it does.
- **Goals.** Only the new airplane icon is used, and picking an icon suggests an example name.
- **Negative amounts** show the sign next to the number, after the currency.
- **Category and due-date alert icons** are now the 3D set.
