# trove 0.7.1

More Brazilian cards, and drawn marks for banks and card networks.

## Cards

- **New banks.** BRB, Safra and Sicredi, each with a standard and a Black version.
- **New versions.** Itaú Personnalité, Bradesco Aeternum and Inter Prime.
- **Marks.** Banks get a small glyph beside the name, and Visa, Elo, Amex and Hipercard are drawn as icons like Mastercard already was. They are simplified shapes, not the official logos.
